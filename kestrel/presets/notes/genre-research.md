## Angelcore (short web research, 2026-11)
Loosely defined late-2010s internet microgenre/aesthetic; sources disagree.
Common thread: airy "heavenly" production (choirs, harps, shimmering pads,
breathy vocals, heavy reverb/delay) with roots in ambient, dream pop and witch
house; sample-pack framing: "dark angelic" electronic style, 108-132 BPM,
"gliding stuttered synth leads", "dark basslines"; a house-leaning strand uses
"warm, melodic bass" with sidechain and gentle saturation.
Sources (aggregator / sample-pack pages, not authoritative):
- https://minatrix.fm/en/mp3/s262-angelcore?page=1
- https://www.loopmasters.com/genres/95-EDM/products/20175-ANGELCORE
- https://pavlosstore.sellfy.store/p/angelcore-bundle-rifkzj/
No source names a specific bass type. Bass recipe starting points (own design):
clean gliding sine sub, saturated gliding "808"-style sine, dark detuned
unison reese, bouncy square house bass, folded-triangle mid bass with mod-wheel
brightness, tempo-gated stutter sub. Keep low end mono and fairly dry.
